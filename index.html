about me
I BRING AN OPERATIONS MINDSET TO PRODUCT DESIGN.
My background in hospitality, travel, and service operations taught me to see where digital experiences meet business reality: exceptions, handoffs, competing priorities, and gaps between how a process is designed and how it actually works. I bring that perspective to product design and UX research, using evidence and collaboration to simplify complex journeys.
I'm a Senior UX Designer with 5+ years of experience in research-led product work and 15+ years in service-heavy environments. Through Zoeh Studio, I support enterprise fintech design as an independent consultant and create digital experiences for service businesses. My previous experience includes UX research at ExxonMobil and product design at Avenue Code (Braskem).
Open to UX & Product Design opportunities worldwide, including U.S. relocation.
